KAYA VC

KAYA VC is the leading early-stage fund in Central & Eastern Europe. Headquartered in Prague with presence in London, Warsaw, Budapest, and San Francisco.

We invest pre-seed to Series A into artificial intelligence, developer tools, infrastructure software, vertical software, healthcare technology, and B2B SaaS. Our portfolio includes unicorns like Rohlik, Docplanner, and Booksy, alongside breakout companies such as Better Stack, E2B, getviktor.com, and Humans&.

Team of 6 experienced investors, who know each other for more than a decade - Martin Rajcan, Tomas Obrtac, Tomas Pacinda, Vaclav Ryslink, Vojtech Tomsu, and Karel Zheng.

Two partners on every deal. You always have someone to call, and we always have someone to challenge our own thinking. We respect founders' time - if it's not a fit, we'll tell you quickly. If it is, we don't need six committee meetings to wire the money.

Fundraising is not a binary outcome for us. In some of our best deals, we invested after we passed 3 times - and we were more than happy to pay up later.
