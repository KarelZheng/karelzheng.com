    Tiny Prague

    Every building, street, tram line, park and the Vltava on this map is drawn from data, in your browser, as you pan. Buildings are extruded from OpenStreetMap footprints; their heights come from the Czech national surface model (DMP) minus the terrain model (DMR 5G), so the hills of Petřín, Hradčany, Letná, Vyšehrad and Žižkov are real.

    Red tiled roofs mark the old city fabric, grey flat roofs the newer blocks, copper the churches. Bridges sit at the height of their abutments; trams run on their own tracks.

    Keys: arrows pan, + / - zoom, N night, L labels. The URL remembers where you are.

    Inspired by isometric.nyc and tiny.london. Data: OpenStreetMap (ODbL), ČÚZK DMR 5G and DMP.
