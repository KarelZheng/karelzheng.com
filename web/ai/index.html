    Tiny Prague, painted

    This version is not drawn from rules. Google's photorealistic 3D model of Prague was rendered from a fixed isometric camera into 1,415 frames of 1024 px, and an image diffusion model repainted all of it as pixel art.

    The model is Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo with the public tiny.london LoRA, trained on London. It needed no Prague training. To avoid seams, the whole city was sampled as one image: every denoising step runs the model on overlapping 1024 px windows and blends their predictions, so neighbouring windows agree on every roof and street.

    The procedural version draws the same city from OpenStreetMap and Czech elevation data in your browser. Button P switches between them at the same spot.
